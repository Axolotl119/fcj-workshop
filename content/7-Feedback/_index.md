# Sharing and Feedback

### What I appreciated about the FCAJ program

* **Learning by building**: the 6-week foundation phase followed by a real team project is far more effective than lectures alone.
* **Mentor support**: timely architecture reviews from Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung kept our design decisions grounded in real-world practice.
* **Community**: weekly sharing sessions in the AWS Study Group exposed me to problems and solutions beyond my own workstream.

### Suggestions for improvement

* Provide a slightly larger AWS credit pool (or a shared sandbox account) — cost anxiety sometimes discouraged experimentation with services like ElastiCache or OpenSearch.
* A mid-program checkpoint demo (around week 8) would push teams to integrate earlier instead of rushing in the final weeks.
* More guidance on **testing** serverless systems (integration testing Lambdas locally, CDK assertions) would round out the curriculum.

### To future interns

Start the cost budget alarm on day 1, delete what you create, read the AWS docs *before* Stack Overflow, and pick a workstream you can own end-to-end — explaining *why* your design works is worth more than the code itself.

**Thank you** to AWS Vietnam, the FCAJ organizers, my mentor, my teammates, and my university supervisor for an unforgettable 12 weeks!
